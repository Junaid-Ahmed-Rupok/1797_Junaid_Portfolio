



## *A-ICF*
## 2026

### • Python, PyTorch,
### • Causal Fairness, Clinical ML
Amortized Invariant Causal Fairness Framework. A five-phase causal fairness auditing framework combining causal-structure discovery, invariant representation learning (DCEVAE), path-specific fairness optimization and a causal decomposition of outcome disparity (TV = SE + IE - DE) for clinical ML. Achieved near-perfect group fairness (Equalized Odds Difference ≤ 0.055) on two clinical datasets (N = 101,766 and N = 4,240), and showed that disparities are driven mainly by spurious/confounding pathways (SE = 0.110 for Framingham) rather than a large direct effect (DE = 0.055). Ablations localized the representation bottleneck. Accepted at IEEE OMLET 2026.

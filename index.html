A-ICF

2026

  • Python
  • PyTorch
  • Causal Fairness
  • Clinical ML

Amortized Invariant Causal Fairness Framework. A five-phase causal fairness auditing framework combining causal-structure discovery, invariant representation learning (DCEVAE), path-specific fairness optimization and a causal decomposition of outcome disparity (TV = SE + IE − DE) for clinical ML. Achieved near-perfect group fairness (Equalized Odds Difference ≤ 0.055) on two clinical datasets (N = 101,766 and N = 4,240), and showed that disparities are driven mainly by spurious/confounding pathways (SE = 0.110 for Framingham) rather than a large direct effect (DE = 0.055). Accepted at IEEE OMLET 2026.

DeepEnMap

2026

  • Python
  • TensorFlow
  • Multi-Modal DL
  • Grad-CAM

Multi-Modal Ordinal Energy Poverty Risk Mapping. Fuses satellite imagery (CNN branch) with demographic data (dense branch) to predict ordinal energy poverty risk, with a custom ordinal-aware loss that penalizes distant misclassifications more heavily. Reached 91.66% accuracy and 0.9102 macro F1; the demographic branch adds under 0.1% compute while improving accuracy by 5.7% over image-only. Accepted at IEMIS 2026 (Springer LNNS).

Hi there

My name is Sarder Junaid Ahmed. I'm an ML researcher and data scientist working on Causal Machine Learning and Trustworthy AI — building systems that reason about cause and effect while staying fair, robust, interpretable and reproducible, applied to domains like criminal justice, environmental governance and healthcare.

I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), and currently work as a Research Assistant at the Center for Artificial Intelligence and Robotics (CAIR), Southeast University, and as a Researcher at Royal Scientific Publications.

Below are details of some of the research software and projects I've built, alongside a look at my published research.

Projects

ReproHub

2024–25

  • Python
  • Streamlit
  • Statistics
  • Reproducibility

Automated Research Reproducibility Platform. Architected a modular platform that automatically verifies published statistical claims against raw data using a composite scoring model (p-value, effect size, test statistic agreement) across 11 statistical tests. Designed a benchmarking suite with synthetic and real-world datasets, and a standardized reporting system that generates automated PDF summaries — reducing manual verification time by 40%.
